**Track:** https://www.datacamp.com/tracks/machine-learning-engineer

---

## 🎯 Engineering Objective

Understand how machine learning models are monitored after deployment to
ensure continued reliability, performance, and business value.

This repository focuses on identifying failure modes in production ML systems
and designing monitoring strategies to detect them early.

---

## 🧠 Core Concepts Covered

- Distinguishing data drift vs. concept drift
- Detecting model performance degradation
- Defining meaningful monitoring and health metrics
- Designing alerting and response strategies
- Connecting monitoring signals to real-world impact

---

## ⚙️ Practical Perspective

Monitoring enables production ML systems to remain effective by supporting:

- early detection of data distribution changes
- ongoing performance validation
- informed retraining decisions
- operational visibility into deployed models

---

## 🔗 Role in ML Engineering Progression

Model monitoring represents the operational layer of the ML lifecycle,
connecting to:

- deployment and lifecycle management
- data validation and quality checks
- experiment tracking and versioning
- continuous improvement of production ML systems
